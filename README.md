
# Stockr

Try it out: https://stockr-game.onrender.com/chart-trainer

Stockr is a suite of tools for stock traders to practice trading stocks in a simulated environment.
Instead of paper trading in real time, you can paper trade historical stock charts and compare your predictions with actual outcomes.




## Demo

https://github.com/shreybirmiwal/stockr-game/assets/67839663/8c31aa97-fe41-4c60-a39b-82d11a5be2df

## Purpose

It's hard to practice trading stocks. You currently have 2 options

* 1.) Paper trade with fake money
This is not ideal since you have to wait for trades to pan out and cannot practice many trades at a time

* 2.) Trading with real money
You can loose real money

The solution is to practice trading on previous historical data, like flashcards! Aim-trainer for stocks.
